How to measure

During an Adams forward bend, place the phone face up, with its long edge across the back (perpendicular to the spine), supported evenly on both sides using an appropriate flat bridge/spacer so the spinous processes do not interfere. Keep the phone steady and screen facing upwards. Tap Start sensor, then Zero on a genuinely level surface, not on the patient. Move along the back and Save reading at the maximum asymmetry for each region.

Clinical note

ATR describes trunk surface asymmetry in the forward-bend position. It is not the radiographic Cobb angle and does not itself diagnose or exclude scoliosis. Findings need clinical context and appropriate follow-up for persistent asymmetry or other concerns. The operator should document standardised positioning and any spacer used. This application has not been clinically validated.

Screening literature often discusses ATR of approximately 5–7° as a consideration for further assessment, but age, examination findings and local guidelines matter. Do not rely on this app alone to make referral decisions.
